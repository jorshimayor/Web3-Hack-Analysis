Exact integer arithmetic (BigInt, 18-decimal token) mirroring the Solidity vaults in Web3-Hack-Analysis. At 9,000 and 15,000 tokens it reproduces the Foundry test results: 0 shares and +9,000 for the attacker on the naive vault, 900 shares with dead shares, 1,799,999 shares with offset 6.

Example contracts are unaudited and for study only. Attack sequence: attacker deposits 1 wei, donates, victim deposits, attacker redeems, victim redeems.
